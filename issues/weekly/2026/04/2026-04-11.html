April 11, 2026 Saturday edition

Amazon confirms $15B AWS AI revenue while agent platforms take shape

Andy Jassy put a $15B annualized number on AWS's AI business and defended $200B in capital expenditure, as AWS, Anthropic, and Perplexity each shipped new agent and finance infrastructure; France committed to a government-wide Linux exit by autumn 2026; and Oxford researchers published an AI tool that catches heart failure risk five years before onset.

What mattered this week

Amazon AWS AI Revenue

Andy Jassy's 2026 shareholder letter disclosed that AWS AI revenue crossed $15 billion annualized in Q1 2026, with Amazon's custom chip business generating over $20 billion annually.

  • AWS AI annual run rate: $15B as of Q1 2026, per CNBC.
  • Custom chip revenue: $20B+ annually; Trainium2 sold out and Trainium3 near full subscription.
  • 2026 capital expenditure: $200B, primarily AI infrastructure, defended on historical demand patterns.
  • Free cash flow declined from $38B to $11B as capital spending rose $50.7B year-over-year.

AWS Agent Registry

AWS launched Agent Registry in preview as part of Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, a centralized discovery and governance platform for AI agents, tools, and skills across enterprise environments.

  • Enterprises can register agents manually or via URL-based discovery from live MCP endpoints.
  • Supports semantic and keyword search by use-case description in natural language.
  • Audit usage via CloudTrail; accessible via console, CLI, SDK, or as an MCP server.
  • Available in five AWS regions at launch.
  • AWS also enabled stateful MCP client capabilities on AgentCore Runtime, supporting user input, LLM sampling, and progress streaming for long-running agent tasks.

Anthropic Advisor Tool

Anthropic released the advisor tool in beta, pairing Opus as an advisory model with Sonnet or Haiku executors in a single API call to improve benchmark performance while cutting costs.

  • Sonnet with Opus advisor gains 2.7 percentage points on SWE-bench while reducing costs by 11.9%.
  • Haiku with Opus advisor scores 41.2% on BrowseComp, up from 19.7% solo, at 85% lower cost than Sonnet alone.
  • Beta header: advisor-tool-2026-03-01; cost capped via max_uses parameter.
  • Full details in the API docs.

Perplexity Finance Hub

Perplexity expanded its Plaid integration beyond investment accounts to checking, savings, credit cards, mortgages, auto loans, and student loans, enabling natural language queries over personal finances.

  • Users can query spending by category, track balances, and generate net worth dashboards.
  • Available on desktop to signed-in US and Canada users; mobile rollout follows.
  • Monthly revenue grew 50%, reaching a $450M annualized run rate, tied to the Computer agent launch in February.
  • Plaid reaches more than half of American bank account holders with over 1M daily connections.

France Exits Windows

France's digital agency DINUM declared an official exit from Windows, requiring each ministry to formalize a Linux implementation plan by autumn 2026.

  • Transition covers desktops, collaboration tools, antivirus, AI platforms, databases, and network equipment.
  • Deadline: autumn 2026 for all ministry implementation plans.
  • 80,000 National Health Insurance Fund employees have already migrated to open-source alternatives to Microsoft Teams, Zoom, and Dropbox.
  • Motivation: reduce dependence on US-based software for digital sovereignty.

Oxford Heart Failure AI

Oxford researchers published an AI tool that predicts heart failure risk at least five years before onset using routine CT scans, detecting microscopic textural changes in cardiac fat tissue.

  • Tested on 70,000+ patients across nine NHS Trusts with decade-long follow-up.
  • Achieved 86% accuracy; the highest-risk group was 20 times more likely to develop heart failure than the lowest-risk group.
  • Published in the Journal of American College of Cardiology.
  • Led by Professor Charalambos Antoniades, University of Oxford; team seeking NHS regulatory approval and expanding to any chest CT.

Research this week

  • GrandCode

    Multi-agent RL system placed first in three consecutive Codeforces live competitions, beating all human participants including grandmasters. Uses Agentic GRPO for multi-stage rollouts.

  • Video-MME-v2

    Updated benchmark for comprehensive video understanding using progressive tri-level hierarchy and group-based evaluation of temporal dynamics and reasoning.

  • InCoder-32B-Thinking

    Industrial code world model using error-driven chain-of-thought synthesis. Scores 81.3% on LiveCodeBench v5 and 84.0% on CAD-Coder.

  • Adam's Law

    Textual Frequency Law: frequent training text should be preferred in both prompting and fine-tuning for LLMs. Introduces Curriculum Textual Frequency Training.
