April 3, 2026 Friday edition

OpenAI makes its first media acquisition, buying live tech show TBPN, as a solo founder hits $1.8B in sales using only AI tools

OpenAI acquires daily live tech talk show TBPN for a reported low hundreds of millions, its first media deal. Separately, Matthew Gallagher built Medvi telehealth to $1.8B projected sales with just $20K and AI tools, fulfilling Altman's 2024 solo-founder prediction. On the policy front, California becomes the de facto testing ground for AI regulation as Newsom signs an executive order and multiple bills advance in Sacramento.

What matters today

OPENAI ACQUIRES TBPN

OpenAI acquired TBPN, a daily live tech talk show that pulls 70,000 viewers per episode and regularly hosts major tech CEOs, in its first media acquisition.

  • Deal reportedly in the low hundreds of millions. Marks OpenAI's first move into media ownership.
  • TBPN provides live distribution and direct access to tech decision-makers, a channel type OpenAI does not currently own.
  • Comes as OpenAI faces growing competition from Anthropic and Google for developer and enterprise mindshare.
  • Raises immediate questions about editorial independence and whether TBPN coverage of competitors will change.

$1.8B SOLO FOUNDER

Matthew Gallagher launched Medvi, a telehealth startup, with $20K and AI tools including ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, and Midjourney, reaching $1.8B in projected sales in 18 months.

  • Fulfills Sam Altman's 2024 prediction that AI would enable solo-founder billion-dollar companies.
  • AI tools replaced entire traditional function teams: design, copywriting, dev, marketing.
  • Telehealth sector well-suited: high-margin, regulation-navigable, replicable patient workflows.

CALIFORNIA AI RULES

Gov. Newsom signed an AI executive order and California legislators advanced multiple AI bills, making the state the de facto national standard-setter for AI regulation.

  • Companies adapt products to California requirements to avoid losing the world's fifth-largest economy as a market.
  • Trump administration is pushing for a preemptive federal framework to prevent state-by-state fragmentation.
  • DOJ also filed an appeal of a federal judge's pause on the Pentagon's Anthropic supply chain risk designation.
