Anthropic opens a research institute and signs a $200M deal with Snowflake

Anthropic launched The Anthropic Institute under co-founder Jack Clark to consolidate its policy and societal research, while a $200 million multi-year partnership with Snowflake embeds Claude Opus 4.5 and Sonnet 4.5 inside enterprise data workflows for 12,600+ global customers.

What matters today

Anthropic Institute

Anthropic launched The Anthropic Institute, a dedicated policy and research body led by co-founder Jack Clark as Head of Public Benefit, consolidating three internal teams under a single structure.

  • Three existing teams absorbed: the Frontier Red Team, the Societal Impacts group, and the Economic Research team.
  • Matt Botvinick, from Yale Law School and Google DeepMind, leads the AI and rule of law research track.
  • Anton Korinek of the University of Virginia heads economic research on labor markets, productivity, and distribution under transformative AI.
  • Anthropic's first Washington DC office opens spring 2026, with Sarah Heck as Head of Public Policy.
  • The Institute will publish research and provide guidance to governments, regulators, and academic researchers during the transition to more powerful AI systems.

Snowflake Partnership

Snowflake and Anthropic announced a multi-year, $200 million expanded partnership to power Claude-based agents inside Snowflake's enterprise data platform for over 12,600 global customers.

  • $200 million multi-year partnership value.
  • Claude Opus 4.5 and Claude Sonnet 4.5 power Snowflake Intelligence, a natural language analytics layer for enterprise data.
  • Snowflake Cortex AI Functions enable multimodal SQL queries spanning text, images, audio, and tabular data with Claude as the reasoning layer.
  • Snowflake Horizon Catalog adds end-to-end governance controls for regulated industries moving from AI pilots to production.
  • Snowflake's customer base: 12,600+ global enterprises.
