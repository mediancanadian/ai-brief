Privacy

What The Median Canadian collects when you subscribe to its email newsletters, the AI Brief (daily) and The Median Canadian (weekly), and what it does with it.

What we collect and why

WhatWhy
Your email addressTo send the newsletter you chose
Which newsletter, and how often if you choseTo send only what you asked for
When and where you subscribed, and your confirmationTo prove you agreed, as Canada's anti-spam law requires
Whether an email was opened or a link clickedTo see which issues and signup forms are useful

Nothing else is asked for. Nothing is sold, rented or shared for advertising. Turning off images in your email app stops the open count.

Who holds it

Subscriber records are kept by our email service, Buttondown, which stores them in the United States (Amazon Web Services, Oregon) and delivers email through providers in the United States. Your information is therefore held outside Quebec and outside Canada, and the laws of the United States apply to it there.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe at any time with the link at the bottom of every email. It takes effect at once.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you by writing to info@mediancanadian.ca.
  • When you unsubscribe, the email service keeps a record that you did, so you are not mailed again by mistake.

Person in charge of personal information

The editor of The Median Canadian, info@mediancanadian.ca.

Sender

The Median Canadian.
